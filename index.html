What stays the same in both states

  1. A free review first

    Send us the address or your notice. We compare the government's value to recent sales, to comparable properties' assessments, and to the income the property produces, and tell you in one page whether a case is worth filing. If the value is already fair, we say so.

  2. We file and argue it

    One signature authorizes us. We file before the deadline, assemble the evidence, handle the assessor's or appraiser's review, and present the case at the hearing. If a settlement is offered, we tell you whether to take it.

  3. You keep most of the savings

    Nothing up front, and nothing if the value doesn't come down. When it does, we invoice a share of the first year's savings. The rest is yours.
